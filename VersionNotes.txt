Version 2.0.0
        Complete overhaul of internal representation of junction support
        All support is one the basis of a fragment which is inferred from the alignments of reads
        This Prevents a Major Bug where a junction could be supported by one read in a pair,
         despite the mate indicating all reads were from PCR duplicates of the same molecule
        Clustering of Candidate regions is now performed via community detection on a graph with junction position nodes
        and edges weighted by levels of support and agreement (enabled via command line option)
